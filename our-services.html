Common insurance questions

Do you help businesses outside Sydney?

Yes. Complete Insurance Advisers NSW is based in Sydney and works with businesses, trades and individuals across Australia. Call or email our brokers to talk about your insurance needs.

Can I ask for a quote if I don’t know what cover I need?

Yes. Select “I’m not sure” on the insurance quote form and tell us what you’d like to protect. A broker can follow up to discuss the options with you.

What is a hard insurance market and how does it affect my business?

A hard insurance market occurs when insurers reduce their capacity to take on risk, often leading to higher premiums, stricter underwriting, and fewer policy options. This can make it harder and more expensive for businesses to secure cover. During a hard market, working with an experienced insurance broker like Complete Insurance Advisors helps you access competitive terms, understand policy changes, and find the right protection despite market pressures.

What does it mean to be underinsured and why is it a problem for businesses?

Being underinsured means your insurance policy’s sum insured isn’t high enough to cover the cost of replacing or repairing your assets after a loss. Many Australian businesses only discover this gap when making a claim. If your cover is outdated or based on old asset values, you could face significant out-of-pocket costs after events like fire, storm, or theft.

How can I reduce the risk of being underinsured?

To avoid underinsurance, review your insurance policies regularly to ensure your sum insured reflects current replacement and reinstatement costs. Update your cover as your business grows, adds new equipment, or expands premises. A qualified insurance broker, such as Complete Insurance Advisors, can assess your risks and help ensure you have adequate protection across property, liability, and business interruption cover.

Why should I use an insurance broker instead of buying insurance myself?

An insurance broker simplifies the complex process of finding the right cover. They compare policies across multiple insurers, ensure you’re not under- or over-insured, and tailor protection to your specific business needs. Like an accountant manages your finances, an adviser manages your insurance so you can focus on running your business.

What are the benefits of working with an insurance broker?

A broker acts for you, not the insurer. They provide expert, accredited advice, access to a broad range of insurance products, and advocate on your behalf during claims. Advisers also help you review and adjust cover as your business evolves, ensuring long-term financial security and compliance.

How does an insurance broker save me money and time?

With strong negotiating power and access to local and global insurers, brokers can secure competitive pricing and better terms than many direct options. They handle research, paperwork, and claims communication — saving you hours of administration while delivering peace of mind that your business is properly protected.
