About us

Sydney insurance brokers you can call

Complete Insurance Advisers NSW helps businesses, trades and individuals across Australia choose cover, review it as their needs change and get support when they make a claim. You can speak to a broker from the first quote through to renewal or claim time.

Who we are

We’re a Sydney-based brokerage with more than 30 years of general insurance experience. We start by asking how you work, what you rely on and what you want to protect. Then we explain your options in plain English so you can decide what cover makes sense for you.

We help with business, trade and personal insurance, including public liability, professional indemnity and commercial motor cover. If you need to claim, we can help with the paperwork, lodge the claim with your insurer and keep you updated.
